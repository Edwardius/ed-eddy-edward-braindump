The parietal lobe is mainly responsible for 
- merging various sensory stimulus together
- mapping internal states to external information and goals. 
- numerical cognition, ability to do math lives here as well

**The inferior parietal lobule has different roles on the right and left brain**
- damage to the left -> apraxia (difficulty doing)
	- **Ideomotor Apraxia** Inability to perform gestures on command
	- **Ideational Apraxia:** Difficulty with sequencing multi-step tasks
- damage to the right -> [[Language and Brain Lateralization|neglect]] (difficulty attending, aware of one's space)
	- blind of the left side

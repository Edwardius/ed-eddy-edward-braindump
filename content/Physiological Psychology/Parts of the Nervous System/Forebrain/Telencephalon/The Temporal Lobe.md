## Superior Temporal Lobe
- contains the #primaryAuditoryCortex and the #secondaryAuditoryCortex 
- responsible for Language Comprehension #naturalLanguageProcessing

## Inferior Temporal Lobe
- visual object **recognition** #perception 
	- [[The Occipital Lobe]] is responsible for **early visual processing**
- Agnosia and Prosopagnosia (inability to recognize objects/faces)

## Medial temporal Lobe
- Emotional Processing, aka #amygdala
- Memory, aka #hippocampus , #worldModeling, #memory

### Cortical Layers
- **Gyri** (bumps) and **sulci** (grooves) increase surface area.
- **Fissures**: deep sulci (e.g., longitudinal fissure, central sulcus, Sylvian fissure).
- **Gray matter**: neuron cell bodies; **white matter**: axons.
- **Gyrus** a bump or potrusion on the brain
- **Sulcus** the groove
- **Bank of Sulcus** the side of a groove
- **Fundus** the bottom of the groove

### Cortical Columns
Aside from layers, the brain is also divided into **columns**
Each column refers to some functional unit.

We divide the cortex by major landmarks:
- Central Sulcus
- Lateral Fissure
- Longitudinal Fissure
- Cingulate Sulcus
- Parieto-occiptal Sulcus

**Key to know**: Sulcus refers to a groove in the brain, Fissue is just a bigger, deeper sulcus lol

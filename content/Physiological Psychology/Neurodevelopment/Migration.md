# Radial Migration 
**Somal Translocation** happens early during the formation of the cortex. Neurons extend themselves to the surface and then their own cell body slurps its way up itself. (like a slug lol) This is purely mechanical.

**Glia-Guided Migration** Is later in the formation of the cortex, where [[Neurons and Neural Cells|radial glia]] structure themselves into scaffolding that other neurons can travel on.

# Tangential Migration
Neurons extending their process in the tangential direction and moving similar to **Somal Translocation**. This is instead guided by concentration gradients of **Chemotropic Cues**. This is guided by chemicals.

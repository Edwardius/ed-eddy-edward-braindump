**Neural Plate** is formed from the [[Neural Induction]] and forms into a **Neural Groove** and **Neural Fold**. The plate begins to fold into itself forming a **Neural Tube**. The center of the tube begins to fill with [[Spinal Cord and Peripheral Nervous System|cerebrospinal fluid]] and becomes the brain ventricles.

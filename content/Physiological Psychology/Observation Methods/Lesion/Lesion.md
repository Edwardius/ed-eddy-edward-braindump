Any disruption or damage of a biological structure.

# Some types
**Aspiration** Removing a segment of the skull, peeling out the meninges, and taking out a portion of the brain. (suction)
**Excitotoxic** Delivering a **neurotoxin** to cause **overstimulation**, killing the neuron.
**Heat** Destroying the brain by overheating it.
**Knife Cut** Cutting out witha knife

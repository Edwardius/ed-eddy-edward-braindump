An advanced form of MRI that visualizes white matter tracts in the brain.

This is done by the differences in behaviours of water in a **unconstrained** and **constrained medium**.

**inside unconstrained medium**:
- Water moves in all directions
**inside constrained medium:**
- Water is biased towards a specific direction

>[!error] DTI measures how water diffuses in tissue. Water diffuses easier along axon bundles, hence revealing tracts.

Uses [[Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI)]] while the participant is doing some sort of task. That task constricts capillaries, causing the overflow of **deoxygenated blood cells (deoxyhemoglobin)** in an area of the brain.

The difference in the concentration can be picked by the MRI machine, and the difference can be used to see areas of increased neuronal activity.

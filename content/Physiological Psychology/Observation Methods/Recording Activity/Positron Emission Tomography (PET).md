Uses radioactive tracers that travel to the brain and burned as part of the brain's metabolism.

**The substance used can be something like fluorodeoxyglucose** which can be used by the brain just like glucose during metabolic activity. 

> [!info] This metabolic activity can be measured by the radioactive substance when used up because a positron is released and, when colliding with an electron, gives off photons which we can pick up
> 

![[Pasted image 20251109195453.png]]

> ![error] The substance must be radioactive/positron-emitting AND also a derivative of a chemical that the brain actively uses (glucose, dopamine, etc. They just have to connect to the right receptors)

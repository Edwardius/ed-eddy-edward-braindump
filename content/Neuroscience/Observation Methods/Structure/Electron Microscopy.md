# Transmission Electron Microscopy (TEM)
Electron beam passes through the specimen and read by a camera array behind a phosphor plate.

# Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM)
Electron beam sent at the specimen in a scanning motion, the amount of backscattered electrons bouncing off the specimen are recorded.

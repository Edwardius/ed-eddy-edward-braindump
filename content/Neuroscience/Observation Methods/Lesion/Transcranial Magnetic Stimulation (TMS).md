Intense mangnetic field is applied on a local area in the brain either activating or deactivate the neurons.

Can make [[Temporary Lesion]].

## Applications
**Repeated TMS (rTMS)** used to treat depression and hallucinations

**Continuous theta-burst stimulation (cTBS**)      **reduces** cortical activity
**Intermittent theta-burst stimulation (iTBS)**      **increase** cortical activity

**Proliferation** refers to rapid cell division creating billions of neurons
**Neurogenesis** refers to the birth of new neurons

> [!error] This is when the brain generates most of the neurons you'll have for life.

Looking at the early cells that form we first start with a bunch of [[Neurons and Neural Cells|Neuroepithelial Cells]], which split off asymmetrically into [[Neurons and Neural Cells|Radial Glial]] and [[Neurons and Neural Cells|Neurons]].

![[Pasted image 20251109220218.png]]

Theres also another form of division which is **Differentiative Division** which create **Basal Progenitors** whose sole purpose is to split into two neurons (just makes neuronal proliferation faster)

![[Pasted image 20251109220434.png]]

This process of Differentiative Division is also related to [[Differentiation and Synaptogenesis]].
Shortly after [[Proliferation and Neurogenesis]] starts, some neurons begin [[Migration]] #neurodevelopment

**Optimization** Find the "best" feasible solution under constraints
$$
\underset{ x \in \mathcal{X} }{ min }f(x)\;\;s.t.\;\;g_{i}(x)\leq 0,\;h_{j}(x)=0
$$
>[!info] Optimization algorithms are search methods

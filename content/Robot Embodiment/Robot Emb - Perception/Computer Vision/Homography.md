A **homography** is any transformation matrix that maps points from one plane to another.

Generally in the form:
$$
\begin{bmatrix}
u' \\
v' \\
1
\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
h_{11} & h_{12} & h_{13} \\
h_{21} & h_{22} & h_{23} \\
h_{31} & h_{32} & h_{33}
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
u \\
v \\
1
\end{bmatrix}
$$

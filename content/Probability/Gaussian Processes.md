Represents a way to represent how a gaussian evolves overtime.
$$
\mathbf{x}(t)=\mathcal{GP}(\boldsymbol{\mu}(t), \boldsymbol{\Sigma}(t,t'))
$$
At a specific time $\tau$, the process devolves to
$$
\mathbf{x}(\tau)=\mathcal{N}(\boldsymbol{\mu}(\tau),\boldsymbol{\Sigma}(\tau,\tau'))
$$

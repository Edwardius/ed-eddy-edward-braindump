Can be useful when multiple two distributions of the same random variable together. Keeps it a distribution adding to 1.

$$
p(\mathbf{x}|\mathbf{y}_{1},\mathbf{y}_{2})=\eta p(\mathbf{x}|\mathbf{y}_{1})p(\mathbf{x}|\mathbf{y}_{2})
$$
$$
\eta=\frac{p(\mathbf{y}_{1})p(\mathbf{y}_{2})}{p(\mathbf{y}_{1},\mathbf{y}_{2})p(\mathbf{x})}
$$
This can be derived from [[Bayes' Theorem]]

A function about a point $a$ can be expanded as:
$$
f(x) = f(a) + f'(a)(x-a) + \frac{f''(a)}{2!}(x-a)^2 + \frac{f'''(a)}{3!}(x-a)^3 + \cdots + \frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!}(x-a)^n + \cdots
$$
or in summation form
$$
f(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!}(x-a)^n
$$
This lets us approximate functions near some operating point $a$ (the farther we are from $a$, the less accurate the approximation).

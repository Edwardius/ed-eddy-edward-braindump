Part of [[Topology]].

# Definition
A **Topological Space** is a pair $(X,\tau)$ where $X$ is a set and $\tau$ is a collection of subsets of X called **open sets**.

# Axioms
1. **Empty set and whole space are open:**
$$
\emptyset \in \tau\;\;\text{and}\;\;X\in \tau
$$
2. **Arbitrary unions of open sets are open:** if $\{ U_{a} \}_{a\in I}$ is any collection of sets in $\tau$, then
$$
⋃_{a\in I}U_{a}\in \tau
$$
3. **Finite intersections of open sets are open:** if $U_{1},U_{2}, \dots,U_{n}\in \tau$ then
$$
U_{1}\cap U_{2}\cap\dots \cap U_{n} \in \tau
$$

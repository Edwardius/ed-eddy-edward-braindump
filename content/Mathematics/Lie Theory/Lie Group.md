A **Lie Group** is a [[Groups|group]] that is also a [[Smooth Manifold]]. Because of this, we can use inherent properties of both [[Groups]] and [[Smooth Manifold|Smooth Manifolds]].

The benefit to this is that, because of the axioms of both Groups and Smooth Manifolds, we can logically deduce special properties that result in [[Lie Algebra]] and the maps that connect the two.

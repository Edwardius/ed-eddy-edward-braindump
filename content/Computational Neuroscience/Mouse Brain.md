**Why do we study the mouse brain?** Its much simpler than the primate brain. There's still a lot we don't know.

![[Pasted image 20260203185231.png]]

The mouse brain has around 40 distinct areas, as opposed to the monkey which has around 130 (note that these areas are rather fuzzy in location and segment).

**Some cortical areas are thought to be #homologous**. That is they share a common evolutionary origin.
